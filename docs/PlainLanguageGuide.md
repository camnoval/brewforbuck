# Plain-Language Guide

*What this app is, for anyone — no technical background needed (§2).*

## The idea

You're at a bar or restaurant looking at a drink menu, and you want the best deal. Not the
cheapest drink — the best *value*: the most alcohol for your money. This app lets you take a
photo of the menu and instantly see the drinks ranked from best value to worst.

## How you'll use it

1. Point your phone at the drink menu and take a picture.
2. The app reads the menu and lists the alcoholic drinks.
3. It ranks them by how much alcohol you get per dollar, best deal first.
4. Anything it had to guess (like the strength of a cocktail or the size of a pour) is
   clearly marked, and you can tap to correct it.

A later version will also let you rank by calories per dollar.

## The one promise we keep

**We never make up a price.** If the app can't clearly read a drink's price, it won't
pretend — it sets that drink aside and asks you to type the price in, rather than ranking it
on a guess. For strength and serving size it will estimate when the menu doesn't say, but it
always shows you that it's an estimate. The whole point is an honest answer: "here's the
best value, and here's exactly what we assumed to get there."

## A note on responsibility

This is an informational price-comparison tool, not an encouragement to drink more. It's for
adults (21+) comparing options, the way you'd compare prices on anything else.
